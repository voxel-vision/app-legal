Streakline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

The short version

Streakline collects no data. None.

Details

Streakline has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no network features. Your habits, schedules, reminder times, and completion history are stored only on your device. Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS and are never sent through a server. Your settings and usage counters are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Contact

Questions: voxel.vision1+streakline@gmail.com

Política de Privacidade (Português)

O Streakline não coleta nenhum dado. Sem contas, sem análises, sem anúncios e sem recursos de rede. Seus hábitos, horários e histórico ficam apenas no seu dispositivo, e os lembretes são agendados localmente pelo iOS. As compras são processadas inteiramente pela Apple.
