Decoy: Imposter Party Game — Privacy Policy

Decoy: Imposter Party Game collects no data. None.

Details

Decoy: Imposter Party Game has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no network features. Word packs are bundled with the app and rounds are played entirely on one device. Your settings and usage counters are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.
