DeepNoise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

The short version

DeepNoise collects no data. None.

Details

DeepNoise has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no network features. All sounds are generated on your device. Your settings and usage counters are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Contact

Questions: voxel.vision1+deepnoise@gmail.com
