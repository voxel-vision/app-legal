KeepAlive — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

The short version

KeepAlive collects no data. None.

Details

KeepAlive has no user accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no network features. All plant data lives on your device (and, if you enable it, in your personal iCloud via Apple CloudKit — we never see it). Your settings and usage counters are stored only on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Contact

Questions: voxel.vision1+keepalive@gmail.com

Política de Privacidade (Português)

O KeepAlive não coleta nenhum dado. Sem contas, sem análises, sem anúncios e sem recursos de rede. Todos os sons são gerados no seu dispositivo. As compras são processadas inteiramente pela Apple.

Política de Privacidad (Español)

KeepAlive no recopila ningún dato. Sin cuentas, sin análisis, sin anuncios y sin funciones de red. Todos los sonidos se generan en tu dispositivo. Las compras son procesadas íntegramente por Apple.
